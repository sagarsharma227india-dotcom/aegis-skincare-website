# AEGIS MEN

### Protection, made simple.

AEGIS MEN is a modern men's skincare platform designed around a simple idea: skincare should be effective without being complicated.

The project combines **science-led skincare, personalized skin guidance, product discovery, routines, and a premium D2C shopping experience** in one interface.

Built with a clinical yet editorial design language, AEGIS MEN focuses on clarity, usability, and consistency rather than visual clutter.

---

## ✦ Overview

AEGIS MEN is designed as a complete digital skincare experience rather than a basic product catalogue.

The platform brings together:

* Personalized skin assessment
* Concern-based product discovery
* Science-focused ingredient information
* Curated skincare routines
* Product detail pages
* Shopping and cart interactions
* Educational skincare content
* Responsive mobile and desktop experiences

The goal is to make choosing skincare feel **simple, informed, and personal**.

---

## ✦ Key Features

### Skin Diagnostic Experience

A guided skin assessment helps users understand their skin concerns and discover a more suitable routine.

### Product Discovery

Users can explore products by category, skin concern, ingredient, and routine-based recommendations.

### Clinical Product Pages

Detailed product views present key actives, benefits, usage information, suitability, pricing, reviews, and supporting information in a structured format.

### Personalised Routines

Users can discover skincare combinations based on common concerns such as acne, oil control, dryness, dullness, and barrier support.

### Science & Education

The platform communicates skincare concepts through ingredient information, educational sections, and science-focused content.

### E-Commerce Experience

Interactive shopping functionality includes product selection, cart interactions, wishlist functionality, promotional offers, and checkout flows.

### Responsive Design

The interface is designed to adapt across desktop, tablet, and mobile screen sizes.

### Micro-interactions

Subtle hover effects, transitions, image scaling, navigation behaviour, and UI feedback are used to create a more refined experience without overwhelming the interface.

## ✦ Design System

AEGIS MEN follows a **Laboratory-to-Editorial** visual direction.

The interface combines clinical precision with premium editorial aesthetics.

### Visual Principles

* Modern minimalism
* Generous whitespace
* Editorial layouts
* Restrained colour usage
* Hairline borders
* Minimal shadows
* Subtle motion
* Strong typographic hierarchy
* Product-focused visual presentation

### Typography

**Playfair Display**
Used for major headlines and editorial moments.

**Inter**
Used for body copy, navigation, labels, and interface elements.

### Colour Direction

* Warm off-white backgrounds
* Deep charcoal typography
* Soft sage primary accents
* Muted blue/lavender scientific accents
* Low-contrast neutral borders

The design system intentionally avoids excessive shadows, heavy gradients, and overly rounded UI elements.

## ✦ Product Experience

The product experience focuses on reducing decision fatigue.

Instead of presenting skincare as a large collection of unrelated products, the platform organizes discovery around:

**Concern → Ingredient → Product → Routine**

This creates a more intuitive path from identifying a problem to building a daily skincare routine.

---

## ✦ Design Philosophy

> **Science × Simplicity**

The product experience is intentionally structured to communicate information without making skincare feel technical or intimidating.

Every section is designed to answer a practical question:

**What is it?**
**Why do I need it?**
**Who is it for?**
**How do I use it?**
